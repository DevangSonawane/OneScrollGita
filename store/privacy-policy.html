Privacy Policy – One Scroll Gita

Last updated: 28 October 2026

One Scroll Gita ("the app") is a free, offline reader for the Bhagavad-gītā As It Is. This policy explains what the app does with information.

We do not collect personal data

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. It does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information. It contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not use the internet: all text is included in the app.

Data kept on your device

Your favourites, bookmarks, reading history, last reading position and settings (language, theme, text size) are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or choosing "Reset all saved data" in Profile, removes them. Your device's own backup service (for example Android backup) may include this data according to your device settings.

Home-screen widgets

If you add the app's widgets, the verse of the day and the verses you read recently are saved on your device so the widgets can show them. They are not sent anywhere.

Sharing

When you choose to share a verse, the app creates a picture and text and passes them to the share sheet of your device. What happens next is controlled by the app you choose to share with.

Install referrer

If you install the app from a shared verse link, Google Play tells the app which verse the link pointed to, so it can open that verse. This information stays on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

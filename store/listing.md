## Full description (4,000 characters max)
Replace a few minutes of scrolling with one verse of the Bhagavad-gītā.

One Scroll Gita brings Śrīla Prabhupāda's Bhagavad-gītā As It Is to your phone in a calm, book-like reader. Open the app, read today's verse, and stop there or keep going. There are no feeds, no streaks, no notifications and no ads.

READ ONE VERSE A DAY
• A new verse every day on the Home screen, the same all day
• Sanskrit in Devanagari, the transliteration and the translation
• Continue where you left off with one tap

SCROLL THROUGH THE WHOLE GITA
• All 18 chapters and 700 verses
• Swipe up for the next verse and down for the previous one
• Jump to any chapter or verse in a tap
• Read Śrīla Prabhupāda's full purports on a quiet, book-like page

ENGLISH AND HINDI
• Switch between English and Hindi (हिन्दी) translations and purports at any time
• The Sanskrit verse is always shown

KEEP AND SHARE WHAT MOVES YOU
• Double-tap a verse to add it to your favourites
• Bookmark verses to find your place again
• Your reading history, kept on your phone
• Tap the Sanskrit, transliteration or translation to copy it
• Share any verse as a beautiful picture card with a link

MADE FOR READING
• Works completely offline. No internet needed, ever
• No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking
• Light and dark themes, five text sizes
• Your favourites, bookmarks and history stay on your phone

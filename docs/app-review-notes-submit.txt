2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
One Scroll Gita is an offline-first reader for the Bhagavad-gita As It Is. It is for people who want to replace a few minutes of phone scrolling with reading one verse a day. Each verse is shown in Sanskrit, transliteration and English or Hindi translation with the full purport, and the app helps readers keep a daily habit, memorise verses and keep personal study notes.

3. HOW TO USE IT
No account, login, credentials or sample files are needed, so no demo account is provided. Signing in is optional: after the welcome screen a "Keep your purchases safe" page offers Sign in with Apple or Google, and "Not now" skips it. Everything in the app works without signing in. To try it, use your own Apple ID with "Continue with Apple" (also under Profile > Account). Profile > Account shows Sign out and Delete account; Delete account removes the account after a confirmation and explains that a subscription is cancelled in the App Store. After the welcome screen the Home tab shows today's verse; tap "Read in Gita" and swipe up/down between verses. All scripture (every verse, translation, purport and word meaning), search, the daily verse and reading reminders are free.
The optional "Sadhak" plan (sadhak_yearly auto-renewable subscription or sadhak_lifetime_ios one-time purchase) unlocks the shloka trainer for any verse, unlimited notes, the journal and PDF export. Open it from the Profile tab (Sadhak card) or by tapping any locked feature. The paywall has Restore Purchases, Terms of Use (Apple standard EULA) and Privacy Policy links.
The optional "Shravan" audio plan (shravan_yearly or shravan_monthly auto-renewable subscriptions, subscription group "Shravan") unlocks listening: Sanskrit shloka recitations verse by verse and a narration of each chapter, with background playback, the lock screen player, a sleep timer and repeat. Open the Listen tab (the round pill beside the main tab bar): the library can be browsed for free, and tapping a chapter or "Unlock Shravan" opens the Shravan paywall, which also has Restore Purchases, Terms and Privacy. Reading the text of every verse never needs Shravan.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
Apple's In-App Purchase (StoreKit) for the Sadhak and Shravan plans. Shravan audio files stream from our own static file host (a Cloudflare Worker serving an R2 bucket at one-scroll-gita-audio.officialaniketjha.workers.dev); no data is sent to it beyond the file request. Supabase Auth (our own project) for the optional Sign in with Apple / Google, account deletion, and a support record of purchases made while signed in (the store stays the only source of truth; nothing is unlocked from it). There are no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no AI services. All texts are bundled in the app, reading works fully offline, and reminders are local notifications.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in all regions; only the plan price differs, as set by the App Store per storefront.
